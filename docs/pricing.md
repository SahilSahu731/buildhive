# BuildHive Pricing (Phase 1)

## Pricing Objectives
- Keep entry friction low for small teams.
- Align price with value delivered (reduced risk + faster review cycles).
- Ensure healthy unit economics with AI processing costs.

## Plans (Initial)

### Free
- Price: $0/month
- Limits:
  - 1 organization
  - 1 repository
  - 50 PR analyses/month
- Features:
  - Basic risk summary
  - Limited history (30 days)

### Pro
- Price: $29/developer/month or $79/repository/month
- Limits:
  - Up to 10 repositories
  - 1,000 PR analyses/month included
- Features:
  - Full severity breakdown
  - Team dashboard
  - Slack notifications
  - 180-day history

### Team
- Price: $199/month (up to 10 developers), then $15 per extra developer
- Limits:
  - 50 repositories
  - 5,000 PR analyses/month included
- Features:
  - Policy templates and enforcement
  - Advanced analytics
  - Priority support
  - 365-day history

## Overage Model
- Additional PR analyses billed at a fixed usage rate.
- Warning threshold at 80% usage.
- Hard cap optional per org to avoid runaway costs.

## Billing Policies
- Monthly and annual billing options.
- 14-day free trial for paid plans.
- 7-day grace period on failed payments.
- Automatic downgrade after grace period if unpaid.

## Revenue Assumptions (Planning)
- Target MRR after 3 months: $2,000-$5,000.
- Expected free-to-paid conversion: 5-8%.
- Gross margin target: above 70% with usage controls.

## Go-To-Market Notes
- Use design partner discounts for first 10 teams.
- Offer annual discount (15-20%) to improve cash flow.
- Highlight ROI through "incidents prevented" and "review time saved" metrics.
